Healthcare Staffing Solutions for Institutions

Kenya Dexters Nursing Agency helps hospitals, clinics, schools, corporate health units, and care facilities access qualified, vetted healthcare professionals according to their exact operational requirements in Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, and Kajiado.

Our 5-Step Approach

  1. 1. Tell Us What You Need: Provide details about the role, required qualifications, number of staff, and assignment duration.
  2. 2. We Understand Your Requirements: We review the operational scope, shift rotations, location, and facility-specific protocols.
  3. 3. We Identify Suitable Professionals: We match appropriate candidates from our qualified network of nurses, clinicians, and therapists.
  4. 4. Verification & Suitability: Professional licenses, regulatory registration (NCK, COC, etc.), background checks, and suitability are strictly verified.
  5. 5. Placement & Coordination: We facilitate smooth onboarding, shift scheduling, and continuous administrative coordination.

Reliable Clinical Support

Whether you require locum coverage, shift replacements, short-term project staffing, or long-term clinical placements, we provide seamless workforce solutions tailored to your organization.
