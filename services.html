Our Services

At Kenya Dexters Nursing Agency, we bring together several areas of healthcare support so that individuals, families, and institutions can access appropriate professional services through one trusted organization across Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, and Kajiado.

Home-Based Care

Professional Care. In the Comfort of Home.

Home can be the best environment for recovery, ageing, and ongoing support when appropriate professional care is available. Our home-based care services are designed to support individuals while allowing them to remain in familiar surroundings.

  • Home Nursing: Professional nursing support for clients who require healthcare assistance at home.
  • Elderly Care: Dedicated assistance for older individuals with daily personal care, wellbeing, and companion support.
  • Palliative Care: Compassionate support focused on comfort, dignity, and quality of life for clients living with serious or life-limiting conditions.
  • Respite Care: Temporary relief support giving primary family caregivers opportunity to rest or attend to other obligations.
  • Post-Hospitalisation Care: Structured transitional care assisting patients returning home after surgery or hospital discharge.
  • Disability & Support Care: Daily assistance designed to promote autonomy for individuals with physical or cognitive disabilities.
  • Nursing Assistance & Caregiver Support: Flexible personal care support tailored around individual client schedules.
