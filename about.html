About Kenya Dexters Nursing Agency

Professionals in Homecare Services

Established in July 2016, Kenya Dexters Nursing Agency provides professional healthcare and homecare services to individuals, families, and institutions. Our services include home-based care, elderly care, palliative and respite care, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, healthcare staffing, and facilitated access to medical expertise through our network of healthcare associates and partners.

We understand that every client is different. Some people need short-term support after hospitalisation. Others require ongoing assistance because of age, disability, chronic health needs, or the need for palliative care. Our approach is therefore centred on understanding each client's needs and helping connect them with appropriate professional support across Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, and Kajiado.

Our Mission

To provide and facilitate access to professional, compassionate, and dignified healthcare services that improve the quality of life of individuals and families.

Our Vision

To become a trusted integrated healthcare organisation recognised for quality homecare, multidisciplinary healthcare services, professional staffing, and dignified care.

Our Values

  • Compassion: We care about the people behind every healthcare need.
  • Dignity: Every person deserves respect and humane treatment.
  • Professionalism: We value competence, responsibility, and professional standards.
  • Integrity: We believe in honest and transparent relationships.
  • Reliability: Families and institutions need professionals they can depend on.
  • Quality: We continually seek better ways to support our clients.

Looking Ahead

Our long-term vision is to grow beyond homecare into a broader integrated healthcare organisation, including the development of a dedicated care facility providing day and residential care, rehabilitation, palliative support, and elderly care. Our ambition is to build a trusted healthcare network that can eventually serve communities across East Africa.
