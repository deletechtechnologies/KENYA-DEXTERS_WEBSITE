Our Professionals

People Who Put Care Into Action

Quality healthcare depends on people. Kenya Dexters Nursing Agency works with vetted healthcare professionals across different disciplines so that clients can access support tailored to their individual needs in Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, and Kajiado.

Professional Categories We Work With

Nurses & Clinicians

Qualified registered nurses and clinical officers equipped to manage medical routines, post-hospitalization care, and vital monitoring at home or in facilities.

Physical & Occupational Therapists

Licensed therapists focused on restoring physical mobility, joint strength, and the ability to perform daily personal activities independently.

Speech Therapists

Experts specializing in evaluating and treating pediatric and adult communication, language development, and swallowing disorders.

Nursing Assistants & Caregivers

Compassionate aides trained in providing essential daily living assistance, elderly care, personal hygiene, and dedicated companionship.

Medical Specialists

Our expansive associate network connects you with specialized healthcare consultants and physicians for complex medical guidance when needed.

Our Commitment to Quality Standards

We strictly consider relevant professional qualifications, valid registration, active licensing, and personal suitability applicable to each role. Our multidisciplinary network allows us to help seamlessly coordinate different areas of support where appropriate.
